Codomax Learning Hub - Student Handbook

About the programme
The Codomax Learning Hub runs virtual internships in Generative AI, Web Development and Data Analytics. Each internship lasts one month and has structured modules with practical tasks.

Attendance and schedule
Students are expected to log in to the internship portal at least five days a week. Each module has a fixed number of days. Module deadlines are shown on the dashboard.

Submission rules
Every module requires a GitHub repository link and a LinkedIn post that tags Codomax Digital Solutions. Later modules also need a live website link. Submissions with broken or private links are not approved.

Certificate policy
A completion certificate is issued only after all modules are submitted and approved by the mentor. The certificate includes a unique ID and a QR code that can be scanned to verify it.

Refund policy
Registration fees can be refunded within seven days of enrolment if no module has been submitted. After that, fees are not refundable.

Support
Students can contact the programme coordinator through the portal. Replies are usually sent within two working days.
